1. Physics-Informed Operator Inference (Constrained Loss Functions)Right now, you are solving a standard Ridge regression to find $\hat{A}$ and $\hat{B}$. You can modify the objective function to explicitly penalize the model if it violates the known log-log slope rules of pressure transient analysis.Instead of just minimizing the state derivative error, you construct a composite loss function:$$ \mathcal{L}_{total} = \mathcal{L}_{data} + \lambda_1 \mathcal{L}_{WBS} + \lambda_2 \mathcal{L}_{MTR} $$$\mathcal{L}_{data}$: The standard snapshot projection error.$\mathcal{L}_{WBS}$: A penalty active only for $t < t_{wbs}$ forcing the Bourdet derivative slope $\frac{d(\log \Delta P')}{d(\log t)}$ to approach $1$.$\mathcal{L}_{MTR}$: A penalty active for $t_{wbs} < t < t_{pss}$ forcing the derivative slope to approach $0$.By solving this using a differentiable physics framework (like PyTorch or JAX) instead of scikit-learn's Ridge solver, the inferred matrices $\hat{A}$ and $\hat{C}$ are mathematically coerced into producing the correct regime shapes.

SVD Projection (Remains Unchanged):You still calculate the SVD on your snapshot matrix $Z$ to get the basis $V_n$.You project your full-order states $X$ into the reduced space: $\hat{X} = V_n^T X$.The operator inference happens purely on $\hat{X}$.The Physics-Informed Loss Function:We need to find the reduced operators $\hat{A}$ (state transition), $\hat{B}$ (input), $\hat{C}$ (output), and $\hat{D}$ (direct feedthrough) that minimize the error in the reduced space, while penalizing unphysical behavior in the full-order reconstructed pressure.Data Loss: $||\dot{\hat{X}} - (\hat{A}\hat{X} + \hat{B}u)||_F^2$WBS Penalty: During early times ($t < t_{wbs}$), the bottom-hole pressure derivative (Bourdet derivative) should have a slope of 1.MTR Penalty: During middle times ($t_{wbs} < t < t_{pss}$), the bottom-hole pressure derivative should have a slope of 0.Guaranteeing Stability:We enforce $\hat{A} = -L L^T$ (where $L$ is lower triangular) to guarantee that the diffusion operator is strictly stable.
